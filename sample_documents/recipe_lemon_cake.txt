Lemon Drizzle Cake

A simple, moist cake that is perfect for a weekend afternoon with tea. Serves 8.

Ingredients: 200g butter, 200g caster sugar, 4 eggs, 200g self-raising flour, zest of 2 lemons, juice of 1 lemon, 100g icing sugar.

Method: Cream the butter and sugar until pale. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then fold in the flour and lemon zest. Bake at 180C for 35 minutes. While it is still warm, mix the lemon juice with icing sugar and pour it over the cake. Let it cool before slicing.
